# Privacy Policy — WinPulse Control Studio

**Effective Date:** November 13, 2026

WinPulse Control Studio is an offline-first Windows desktop utility. We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal or diagnostic data.

## What the App Reads Locally
To display system status and run user-initiated diagnostics or optimizations, the app may read local information on your device, including:
- Operating system, hardware, CPU core count, and RAM usage telemetry
- Running process lists and service states
- Windows Event Logs and driver/system health reports

## Local Storage
The app stores your preferences and operational logs strictly on your local machine under `%LOCALAPPDATA%\WinPulseStudio`:
- Run history (`history.json`)
- Pinned favorites (`favorites.json`)
- Custom script collections (`collections.json`)
- Edited script overrides (`scripts\`)

## Data Transmission
**Nothing leaves your PC.** WinPulse Control Studio does not include analytics, telemetry SDKs, advertising trackers, or cloud sync.
